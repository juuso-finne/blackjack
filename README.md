# Blackjack  

## Rules / How to play  
The goal of the game is to get a higher score than the dealer without going over 21 ("busting"). A numbered card's point value is its face value. A face cards' point value is always 10. An ace's point value can be either 1 or 11.  
  
Increase or decrease your bet by pressing the "Bet +" or "Bet -" buttons, respectively. Start the round by pressing the "Deal" button.  
At the beginning of the round, you are dealt two cards and the dealer is dealt one card face-up. If the dealer's card is an ace, you can take insurance. This will be explained later.  
If your two starting cards are an ace and a ten-point-card, you have a blackjack. If the dealer doesn't have blackjack, you win 1.5 times your bet. If the dealer also has blackjack, you don't win or lose ("push").  
There are four player actions, each mapped to their respective button:  
- **Split:** This action is available only if your hand has exactly two cards and they have the same point value. Your hand is split into two hands, both of which are dealt a card. You place a bet equal to your original bet on the new hand. Both hands are played separately. The hand that is being played is highlighted while other hands are greyed out.  
You are allowed to split once more during the same round, making the maximum number of hands per round four. If you split aces, the hands don't get any additional cards. A split hand is *never* a blackjack.  
- **Double:** This action is available only if your hand has exactly two cards. You double your original bet for the hand in play. The hand is dealt one card and no more.  
- **Hit:** The hand is dealt additional card. If the hand's score is greater than 21, the hand busts and loses immediately.  
- **Stand:** The turn is passed to the next hand or the dealer.

It is now the dealer's turn. The dealer must hit if their point total is less than 17, with all the aces counting as 1 point. The dealer's score is compared to all player hands one by one. If the dealer's score is higher, the player's hand loses.  
If the dealer's score is equal, it's a push. If the dealer's score is lower or dealer busts, player wins an amount equal to the bet. Blackjack always wins a non-blackjack.  
  
Insurance is a side bet that is offered if the dealer's first card is an ace. Its outcome neither depends on nor affects player hands' outcomes. The insurance is half of the original bet. If the dealer does have blackjack, the player wins an amount that is double the insurance.
